# Community Code of Conduct

**English** · [한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ko.md)

[Project overview](README.md) · [Contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Security policy](SECURITY.md)

DevLink aims to be a community where people can ask questions, learn, and contribute with mutual respect. This code applies to collaboration spaces managed by the project, including repository Issues, Pull Requests, and code reviews.

## Expected behavior

- Respect others regardless of their experience, nationality, language, gender, disability, or other personal background.
- When you disagree, focus on proposals and code rather than individuals, and explain your position with specific evidence.
- Treat questions and mistakes as part of learning, and give feedback that helps others improve.
- Protect other people's personal information and private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, discrimination, sexual harassment, threats, and persistent harassment
- Disclosure of personal information or private conversations without consent
- Repeated spam, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption of collaboration

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or observe a problem, contact the repository maintainers. If you do not know how to contact them privately, you may use [Issues](https://github.com/SSAFY-URAYAHA/DevLink_server/issues) to request a private contact method without describing the incident. Do not post a reporter's identity, personal information, or sensitive evidence in a public Issue.

Maintainers may review the situation and take action, such as requesting edits or removal of posts, issuing warnings, or restricting participation in project spaces. Decisions will consider the severity and recurrence of the behavior.

Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) to report security vulnerabilities. See the [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development participation.
